# Vision

Giving should remain understandable after the transaction.

AGI exists to make the path from funding intent to verified public outcome legible without collapsing recommendation, execution, evidence, and attribution into one claim. Portfolio Signals improves decision visibility, AGI explains the journey, and Impact Relay preserves approved aggregate evidence.

## Principles

- A receipt proves a transaction, not an outcome.
- Evidence must retain provenance, authority, and limitations.
- Missing information must remain missing rather than becoming a generated claim.
- Donor privacy and beneficiary dignity are product requirements.
- Human approval remains mandatory for consequential financial and publication decisions.
- Shared suite identity must not erase distinct system responsibilities.

The near-term product remains a public, deterministic, read-only experience. Real-time data, authenticated workflows, payments, and notifications are future capabilities that require stronger governance—not assumptions hidden inside the demo.
